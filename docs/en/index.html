How it works

From zero to your first server in 3 steps

Made for people who don't want to learn networking just to play with friends.

1

Download and install

Download the installer and open Cubicase. No network setup, no administrator password.

2

Create your server

Pick the Minecraft version and the mod loader (Forge, Fabric or NeoForge) — Cubicase takes care of Java and the whole installation.

3

Share the code

Send a short code to your friends. They paste it into their Cubicase and jump right into the world.

Features

Everything a server host needs

Without having to configure your home router or pay for hosting.

No port forwarding

A private (mesh) network connects you and your friends directly, without exposing your home router to the internet.

A few clicks until you play

Cubicase downloads the right Java and the server and sets everything up on its own — you just pick the version.

Real-time diagnostics

Detects lag, crashes and resource bottlenecks before they ruin everyone's session.

Updates itself

Every improvement arrives automatically in the background — no need to download the installer again.

Ready to play with the crew?

It takes less than 5 minutes from download to your first server online.
